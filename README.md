# Employee & HR Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes employee and HR data to evaluate workforce distribution,salary costs,attendance, leave usage, and employee performance. THE ANALYSIS AIMS TO IDENTIFY KEY hr trends and provide actionable business recommendations to support better workforce management and decision-making.
## Business Problem/ Client Requirement
The HR team wants to better understand workforce distribution,employee costs,attendance,leave usage,and performance across departments.
The objective is to use SQL analysis to identify important HR trends,highlight areas that may require management attention,and provide actionable recommendations for improving workforce management and employee performance.
## Project objectives
. Analyze employee distribution across different departments.
. Evaluate employee salary and cost patterns.
. Analyze attendance and leave trends.
. Identify employee performance patterns.
. Generate actionable HR insights and recommendations using SQL analysis.
## Tools & Technologies
. Python
. SQlite
. SQL
. VS Code
. Git & GitHub
## Data Analysis
The project uses SQL queries to analyze employee and HR data across multiple business areas,including:
. Employee distrubution across departments 
. Salary and employee cost analysis
. Attendance analysis
. Leave usage and leave types
. Employee performance analysis 
. Identification of key HR trends and patterns
## Key Insights
- Finance has the highest number of employees.
- HR has the highest average salary, while Marketing has the lowest. 
- Finance and Sales have the highest attendance rates.
- Leave usage is relatively cosistent across departments.
- Most employees have satisfactory performance, with some requiring improvement.
## Business Recommendations
- Moniter employee costs across departments.
- Maintain strong attendance levels and address attendance concerns.
- provide additional support and development opportunities for employees who need improvement.
