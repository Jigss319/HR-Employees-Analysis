# Business Recommendations

### 1. Improve Employee Attendance
Identify employees and departments with lower attendance rates and introduce attendance monitoring and engagement initiatives to improve workforce availability.

### 2. Support Employee Performance
Provide targeted training,mentoring, and regular feedback to employees with low performance scores or "Need improvement" comments.

### 3. Monitor Department-Level HR Costs
Review departments with higher salary costs alongside attendance and performance metrics to ensure employee costs are aligned with productivity and business needs.
